# Privacy policy

Clementine Remote controls the Clementine music player over your local network. It does not
collect, store or share any personal data, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

- **Local network.** The app only connects to the Clementine you choose, found on your local
  network or by its address. iOS asks you before the app can look for it.
- **Storage.** The app keeps the address of your Clementine and its settings on your device.
  Songs you download from Clementine are saved in the app's folder, which you can see in the
  Files app.
- **Links** in the About screen open in your browser.

Nothing leaves your device except the commands and requests sent to your own Clementine.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/clementine-player/iOS-Remote/issues
