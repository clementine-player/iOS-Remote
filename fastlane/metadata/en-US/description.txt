Clementine Remote controls the Clementine music player on your computer from your iPhone or iPad, over your home Wi-Fi.

Features
• Play, pause, skip, seek and change the volume, also with your phone's volume buttons
• Browse your library and playlists, and search for songs
• Browse Clementine's internet services: radio stations, Jamendo, Subsonic, Plex and more
• See the cover art and read the lyrics
• Rate songs, and switch shuffle and repeat
• Download songs, albums or whole playlists to your phone, no cable needed
• Play Clementine's music on your phone instead of your computer, with controls on the lock screen
• A widget to play, pause and skip from your Home Screen
• Finds Clementine on your network by itself, or connect by address

Setup
You need Clementine 1.3 or later. In Clementine, open Tools → Preferences → Network Remote and turn on "Use a network remote control". To download songs, also allow downloads there.

Clementine Remote is free software (GPLv3). It collects no data: it only talks to your own Clementine, on your own network.
