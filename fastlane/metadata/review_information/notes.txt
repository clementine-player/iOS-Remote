Clementine Remote is a remote control for Clementine, a free music player for macOS, Windows and Linux. It needs a Clementine to connect to.

For review, there's a Clementine on the internet, playing a small library of public-domain works (the audio is a generated tone). Its address and auth code are below. To test the app with it:
1. Open Clementine Remote. Under "Or enter its address", enter the address below and tap Connect.
2. When the app asks for an auth code, enter the one below.
3. The queue, the player, the library, search, Clementine's internet services and downloads all work with it.

To test with Clementine on your own computer instead: install it from https://www.clementine-player.org/, open Tools → Preferences → Network Remote, turn on "Use a network remote control" (and allow downloads), and connect from a device on the same Wi-Fi network. The app finds it by itself.

The app has no accounts and collects no data.
