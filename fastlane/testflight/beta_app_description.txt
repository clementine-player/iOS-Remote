Clementine Remote controls the Clementine music player on your computer from your iPhone or iPad, over your home Wi-Fi.

You need Clementine 1.3 or later on a computer on the same network. In Clementine, open Tools → Preferences → Network Remote and turn on "Use a network remote control". To download songs, also allow downloads there. To play Clementine's music on your phone, which needs a recent Clementine, also turn on "Allow playing on remote devices".

Things to try:
• Connecting: the app finds Clementine by itself, or enter its address.
• Playing, pausing, skipping, seeking and the volume, also with your phone's volume buttons.
• Browsing the library, playlists and Clementine's internet services, and searching.
• Downloading songs, albums and playlists to your phone.
• Playing Clementine's music on your phone instead of your computer, from the button in the mini player, with the controls on the lock screen.
• The widget on your Home Screen.

Builds come from every change to the app, so some will have rough edges. To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and share it with TestFlight, or use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
